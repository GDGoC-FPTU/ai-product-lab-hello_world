# AI Log - Reflection

Trong buoi lab nay, minh dung AI nhu mot `thought-partner` hon la nguoi lam bai thay minh. O giai doan dau, minh dung AI de brainstorm nhanh cac pain point cho ba don vi `Vinmec`, `VinFast`, va `Vinpearl`, sau do nho AI giup chuyen y tuong tho thanh cac `quick problem cards` co actor, workflow, bottleneck va metric cu the. AI dac biet huu ich khi minh chua ro nen chon bai toan nao de deep-dive: no giup minh so sanh giua `Vinmec discharge summary`, `VinFast warranty triage`, va `Vinpearl review triage`, tu do nhin ra bai toan Vinpearl co du lieu vao de lay hon, rui ro van hanh thap hon, va phu hop de lam prototype trong thoi gian ngan. Ngoai ra, minh cung dung AI de ho tro viet `system prompt`, dinh dang `JSON output`, nghi ra `adversarial test cases`, va sua mot so loi moi truong Python khi chay prototype.

Tuy nhien, AI khong phai luc nao cung dung. Mot diem minh thay ro la AI ban dau rat de bi "ham" bai toan co impact lon, nen nghieng ve `Vinmec discharge summary` vi nhin thay tiet kiem nhieu thoi gian cho bac si. Van de la de xuat do chua tinh du cac rui ro thuc te nhu `PHI`, quy trinh phe duyet y khoa, va yeu cau truy cap EMR noi bo. Mot sai lech khac la khi draft use case cho `Vinpearl`, AI ban dau qua lac quan ve kha nang tu dong hoa, de xuat phan hoi review theo kieu tong quat ma chua khoa chat cac tinh huong nhay cam nhu `food poisoning`, `refund`, `injury`, hay `legal complaint`. Neu tin nguyen ban dau cua AI, he thong de bi vuot ranh gioi van hanh va tra loi qua tham quyen.

Sau khi thay nhung diem yeu do, minh da sua prompt theo huong cu the hon va "ep" AI lam viec trong khung hep. Minh bo sung cac ranh gioi ro rang: moi `public reply` deu phai bat dau bang `[DRAFT_ONLY]`; AI khong duoc hua `refund`, `voucher`, `upgrade`, hay xac nhan su that khong co trong input; bat ky review nao co keyword `safety`, `hygiene`, `injury`, `refund`, `media`, `legal` deu phai tra ve `action = escalate_to_manager`; va output phai la `JSON` co cau truc de de kiem tra bang rule-based logic. Cach lam nay giup minh nhan ra mot bai hoc quan trong: AI manh o viec mo rong y tuong va viet nhap nhanh, nhung de dung duoc trong van hanh that thi con nguoi van phai dat `boundary`, `HITL`, va `fallback` rat ro.
